# Archive (the adopted estate)

Written by `flows/adopt.md` (pass 0). One `inventory-entry` per artifact
found in the existing estate, plus `BACKLOG.md`, the shopping list of
dispositions (`migrate` / `merge` / `keep-as-is` / `retire`).

Rules:
- The archive is the only space where non-conforming artifacts are admitted:
  entries record what IS, frozen, and are never edited to look better.
- Entries are not registry members and are not indexed in `wiki/INDEX.md`;
  `BACKLOG.md` is their local index.
- A `migrate` disposition is executed as a normal pass-1 creation seeded
  from the entry. The entry stays here afterwards, marked `migrated`, as the
  provenance trace.
